# EigenLayer Protocol Case Study: Dollar Distribution Analysis

## Overview

This case study analyzes how every dollar generated by EigenLayer protocol (restaking) is distributed across core recipients based on official EigenLayer documentation and on-chain data.[^1] EigenLayer has pioneered the concept of restaking, enabling ETH stakers to extend cryptoeconomic security to additional protocols and services while earning additional rewards.[^7]

**🔷 HARD DATA (January 2, 2026):**
- **Total Value Locked**: $12.74 billion[^2]
- **EIGEN Token Price**: $0.372[^5]
- **EIGEN Market Cap**: $200.51 million[^5]
- **EIGEN FDV**: $663.34 million[^5]
- **EIGEN Circulating Supply**: 538.62 million[^5]
- **Active AVS Protocols**: 20+[^21]
- **Registered Operators**: 200+[^13]

EigenLayer enables the creation of Actively Validated Services (AVSs) that can leverage Ethereum's pooled security without bootstrapping their own validator sets.[^10] The protocol has grown to become the largest restaking infrastructure, serving as the foundation for data availability layers, bridges, oracles, and other middleware services.[^21]

## Revenue Structure

### Restaking Rewards
- **Recipient**: Restakers and operators
- **Mechanism**: ETH staking yield + AVS rewards
- **Distribution**: Pass-through to stakers

### AVS (Actively Validated Services) Fees
- **Recipient**: Operators and restakers
- **Mechanism**: Service-specific fee structures
- **Distribution**: Based on validation work

### Slashing Penalties
- **Recipient**: Affected AVS protocols
- **Mechanism**: Compensation for misbehavior
- **Distribution**: Penalty from slashed operators

### Protocol Fees (Future)
- **Recipient**: EigenLayer protocol (not active)
- **Mechanism**: Potential fee on rewards
- **Distribution**: To be determined by governance

## Economic Flow Breakdown

### Revenue Generation Lifecycle
1. Users deposit ETH/LSTs for restaking
2. Assets delegated to operators
3. Operators validate for multiple AVSs
4. AVS rewards distributed to operators
5. Operators share with restakers
6. Liquid restaking protocols take fees

### Revenue Distribution Percentages
- **Restakers**: ~70-85% of total rewards
- **Operators**: ~10-20% commission
- **Liquid Restaking Protocols**: ~5-10% fees
- **AVS Protocols**: Variable service fees
- **EigenLayer Protocol**: 0% currently
