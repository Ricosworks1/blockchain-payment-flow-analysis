# Lido Protocol Case Study: Dollar Distribution Analysis

## Overview

This case study analyzes how every dollar generated by Lido protocol is distributed across core recipients based on official Lido documentation and on-chain data.[^1] Lido has established itself as the dominant liquid staking protocol on Ethereum, enabling users to stake ETH while maintaining liquidity through stETH tokens.[^2]

Lido's stETH has become foundational infrastructure for DeFi, serving as collateral across major lending protocols and liquidity pools.[^29] The protocol operates with a 10% fee structure split equally between node operators and the DAO treasury, providing sustainable funding for protocol development and operator incentives.[^16]

## Revenue Structure

### Protocol Fee
- **Recipient**: Split between Node Operators and Lido DAO Treasury[^16]
- **Mechanism**: 10% of staking rewards (5% + 5% split)
- **Distribution**: 5% to 30+ curated node operators, 5% to DAO treasury[^8]

### Staking Rewards
- **Recipient**: stETH holders[^1]
- **Mechanism**: 90% of Ethereum staking rewards pass-through
- **Distribution**: Auto-compounded via daily rebasing to stETH holders[^6]

### MEV Rewards
- **Recipient**: Distributed through protocol fee structure[^45]
- **Mechanism**: MEV-Boost integration with relay operators
- **Distribution**: Same 90/10 split as base consensus rewards[^45]

### Execution Layer Rewards
- **Recipient**: Protocol and stETH holders[^7]
- **Mechanism**: Priority fees from transaction ordering
- **Distribution**: Included in total reward calculation

## Core Recipients Analysis

### 1. stETH Holders (Liquidity Providers)
- **Revenue Sources**:
  - 90% of all staking rewards
  - 90% of MEV rewards
  - Auto-rebasing token appreciation
- **Distribution Mechanism**:
  - Daily rebasing
  - Proportional to holdings

### 2. Node Operators
- **Revenue Sources**:
  - 5% of all staking rewards
  - 5% of MEV rewards
  - Performance-based allocation
- **Distribution Mechanism**:
  - 30+ professional operators
  - Stake-weighted distribution

### 3. Lido DAO Treasury
- **Revenue Sources**:
  - 5% of all staking rewards
  - 5% of MEV rewards
  - LDO token holdings
- **Distribution Mechanism**:
  - DAO governance control
  - Development funding

### 4. LDO Token Holders
- **Revenue Sources**:
  - No direct fee distribution
  - Governance rights only
- **Distribution Mechanism**:
  - Voting power
  - Treasury management rights

### 5. Oracle Operators
- **Revenue Sources**: Operational grants from treasury
- **Mechanism**: Price feed maintenance
- **Distribution**: Service compensation

### 6. Development Teams
- **Revenue Sources**: Grants from DAO treasury
- **Mechanism**: Development proposals
- **Distribution**: Milestone-based payments

### 7. Insurance Fund
- **Revenue Sources**: Portion of treasury allocation
- **Mechanism**: Slashing protection
- **Distribution**: Reserved for coverage events

## Economic Flow Breakdown

### Revenue Generation Lifecycle
1. Users deposit ETH, receive stETH
2. ETH staked with node operators
3. Validators earn rewards + MEV
4. 10% fee taken from rewards
5. 90% distributed to stETH holders
6. 5% to operators, 5% to treasury

### Fee Distribution Percentages
- **stETH Holders**: 90% of all rewards
- **Node Operators**: 5% of all rewards
- **DAO Treasury**: 5% of all rewards
- **LDO Holders**: 0% direct distribution
