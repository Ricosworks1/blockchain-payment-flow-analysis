## Revenue Structure

### Morpho Blue Interest Spread[^7][^38]
- **Recipient**: Morpho Blue market participants[^7][^27]
- **Mechanism**: Interest rate spread between lenders and borrowers[^38]
- **Distribution**: 100% to users (0% protocol fee currently)[^26]

### MetaMorpho Vault Fees[^14][^41]
- **Recipient**: Vault curators[^14][^42][^43]
- **Mechanism**: Performance fees on vault yields[^41]
- **Distribution**: 2-10% of yield to curators[^14], remainder to depositors[^28]

### Peer-to-Peer Interest Optimization (Optimizer)[^13][^25]
- **Recipient**: Matched lenders and borrowers[^13]
- **Mechanism**: Direct P2P matching, eliminating spread[^25][^37]
- **Distribution**: Better rates for both sides[^37]

### MORPHO Token Rewards[^46][^47]
- **Recipient**: Users and liquidity providers[^46]
- **Mechanism**: Incentive distribution program[^46][^47]
- **Distribution**: Usage-based allocation via Age system[^47]

## Economic Flow Breakdown

### Morpho Blue Market Lifecycle[^7][^27]
1. Anyone creates lending market (permissionless)[^7]
2. Lenders supply assets to market[^27]
3. Borrowers post collateral and borrow[^40]
4. Interest paid directly to lenders[^38]
5. 0% protocol fee extraction[^26]
6. Liquidations if health factor drops[^40]

### MetaMorpho Vault Flow[^14][^41]
1. Users deposit into curated vaults[^28]
2. Curators allocate to Morpho Blue markets[^14][^42]
3. Risk-adjusted returns generated[^29][^43]
4. Performance fees to curators (2-10%)[^41]
5. Net yield to vault depositors[^28]

### Fee Distribution Percentages[^1][^26]
- **Morpho Blue Lenders**: 100% of interest (no protocol fee)[^26]
- **Vault Depositors**: ~90-98% of vault yields[^14][^41]
- **Vault Curators**: ~2-10% performance fees[^14][^42]
- **Protocol**: 0% (fee switch inactive)[^26]
