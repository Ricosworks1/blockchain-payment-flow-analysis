# Pendle Finance Case Study: Dollar Distribution Analysis

## Overview

This case study analyzes how every dollar generated by Pendle Finance[^6] (yield tokenization protocol)[^6] is distributed across core recipients based on official documentation[^6] and on-chain data[^7][^9].

**Key Metrics (December 31, 2025):** 🔷 HARD DATA (retrieved via DefiLlama[^7] and CoinGecko[^8] APIs)

- **PENDLE Token:** $2.00 | Market Cap: $333M | FDV: $564M[^8]
- **Circulating Supply:** 166.2M PENDLE (59%)[^8]
- **24h Volume:** $45.6M[^8]
- **vePENDLE Staked:** $132.8M[^7]
- **Chains:** Ethereum, Arbitrum, Plasma, BNB, Base, Hyperliquid, Sonic, Mantle, Avalanche, Optimism, Berachain[^7]

## Revenue Structure

### Yield Token (YT) Fees[^2][^40]
- **Recipient**: vePENDLE stakers[^2][^51]
- **Mechanism**: 3% of accrued yield on YT tokens[^40]
- **Distribution**: 100% to vePENDLE holders[^2][^51]

### Swap Fees (AMM Trading)[^2][^39]
- **Recipient**: Liquidity providers and vePENDLE stakers[^2]
- **Mechanism**: 10-30 bps per transaction[^2][^39]
- **Distribution**: 50% to LPs[^41], 50% to vePENDLE[^51]

### Protocol Fee (Currently 0%)[^2]
- **Recipient**: Treasury (inactive)[^2]
- **Mechanism**: Potential future fee on revenue[^2]
- **Distribution**: Currently 100% to users[^2]

### PENDLE Emissions[^41]
- **Recipient**: Liquidity providers[^41]
- **Mechanism**: Weekly PENDLE distribution[^41]
- **Distribution**: Based on LP position size and vePENDLE boost[^41][^15]

## Core Recipients Analysis

### 1. vePENDLE Stakers[^15][^51]
- **Revenue Sources**:
  - 100% of YT fees (3% of yield)[^2][^40]
  - 50% of swap fees[^2][^51]
  - Governance voting rights[^21][^22]
  - LP reward boosts[^41][^15]
- **Distribution Mechanism**:
  - Lock PENDLE for 1 week - 2 years[^15]
  - Longer lock = more vePENDLE[^15]
  - Pro-rata revenue distribution[^51]

### 2. Principal Token (PT) Buyers[^36]
- **Revenue Sources**:
  - Fixed yield at maturity[^36]
  - Discount to face value[^36]
- **Costs**: Swap fee on purchase (10-30 bps)[^2]
- **Distribution Mechanism**:
  - Buy PT at discount[^36]
  - Redeem 1:1 at maturity[^36]
  - No ongoing fees[^36]

### 3. Yield Token (YT) Buyers[^37]
- **Revenue Sources**:
  - All future yield from underlying[^37]
  - Leveraged yield exposure[^37]
- **Costs**: 3% yield fee[^40] + swap fee[^2]
- **Distribution Mechanism**:
  - Continuous yield distribution[^37]
  - Time decay to zero at maturity[^37]

### 4. Liquidity Providers[^41]
- **Revenue Sources**:
  - 50% of swap fees[^2][^41]
  - PENDLE emissions[^41]
  - External incentives[^41]
- **Distribution Mechanism**:
  - Deposit PT + Underlying into AMM[^39]
  - Earn continuous fees + rewards[^41]
  - vePENDLE boost available[^15][^41]

### 5. Pendle Treasury[^2]
- **Revenue Sources**:
  - Currently $0 (0% fee)[^2]
  - Potential future allocation[^2]
- **Distribution Mechanism**:
  - Governance-controlled[^21][^22]
  - May activate 20% share in future[^2]

### 6. Underlying Protocol Teams[^27][^28][^29][^30]
- **Revenue Sources**:
  - Integration partnerships[^27]
  - Co-incentives with Pendle[^41]
- **Examples**: Ethena USDe[^27], Lido stETH[^28], Aave[^29], Compound[^30]

## Economic Flow Breakdown

### Yield Tokenization Lifecycle[^6][^36][^37]
1. User deposits yield-bearing asset[^38]
2. Wrapped into SY (Standardized Yield)[^38]
3. SY mints PT + YT tokens[^36][^37]
4. PT = principal at maturity[^36]
5. YT = all yield until maturity[^37]
6. YT holders pay 3% yield fee[^40]
7. PT holders pay no ongoing fees[^36]

### Fee Distribution Percentages[^2][^51]
- **vePENDLE Stakers**: 100% of YT fees + 50% swap fees[^2][^51]
- **Liquidity Providers**: 50% of swap fees + emissions[^41]
- **PT Holders**: 0% fees (fixed yield)[^36]
- **YT Holders**: 3% fee on yield[^40]
- **Treasury**: 0% (currently)[^2]

## PT/YT Mechanics

### Principal Token (PT)[^36]
- Represents underlying at maturity[^36]
- Trades at discount before maturity[^36]
- Discount = implied fixed yield[^36]
- No ongoing fees[^36]
- Risk: Opportunity cost only[^36]

### Yield Token (YT)[^37]
- Represents all future yield[^37]
- Trades based on expected yield[^37]
- 3% protocol fee on yield[^40]
- Time decay to zero[^37]
- Risk: Can lose 100%[^37]

### Standardized Yield (SY)[^38]
- Wrapper for yield-bearing assets[^38]
- Standardizes different protocols[^38]
- Enables PT/YT minting[^38]
- Supports: stETH, sUSDe, aTokens, etc.[^27][^28][^29][^30]
