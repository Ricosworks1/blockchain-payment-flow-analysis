## What Are Prediction Markets

### Core Concept

Prediction markets are **financial markets created for the purpose of trading the outcome of future events**[^26]. Participants buy and sell shares that pay out based on whether specific outcomes occur, with market prices reflecting the collective probability assigned by traders[^27].

**Basic Mechanism:**

```
User deposits $100 USDC
    ↓
Buys "YES" shares for event: "Bitcoin above $100k by Dec 31, 2025"
    ↓
Current price: $0.60 per share (implies 60% probability)
    ↓
Buys 166 shares ($100 / $0.60)
    ↓
Outcomes:
- If BTC > $100k: Receives $166 (each share pays $1)
- If BTC < $100k: Receives $0 (shares expire worthless)
```

### Types of Prediction Markets

**1. Binary Markets** (Yes/No outcomes)[^28]
- "Will Trump win 2024 election?" → YES or NO
- Each outcome token represents probability
- Payoff: $1 if correct, $0 if wrong

**2. Categorical Markets** (Multiple exclusive outcomes)[^29]
- "Who will win the Super Bowl?" → Team A, Team B, Team C, etc.
- Only one outcome can occur
- Winning outcome pays $1, others $0

**3. Scalar Markets** (Range of values)[^30]
- "What will ETH price be on Dec 31?" → Ranges ($1000-$1500, $1500-$2000, etc.)
- Allows betting on price ranges or continuous outcomes

### Why They Work: Aggregated Intelligence

**Wisdom of the Crowds:**

Prediction markets harness the **wisdom of crowds** principle: aggregated predictions from diverse participants often outperform expert forecasts[^31].

**Historical Accuracy:**

- **Iowa Electronic Markets:** Outperformed polls in 75% of presidential elections (1988-2020)[^32]
- **PredictIt:** Within 2-3% of actual election outcomes on average[^33]
- **Sports Betting Markets:** Consistently more accurate than expert predictions[^34]

### Conditional Token Framework (CTF)

**What is CTF:**

CTF is an open-source smart contract framework for creating prediction markets[^139]:

1. **Outcome Tokenization:** Converts collateral into YES/NO shares[^140]
2. **Position Splits:** Enables complex conditional logic[^141]
3. **Payout Logic:** Automatic distribution and settlement[^142]

**How CTF Works:**

```
User deposits 100 USDC
    ↓
CTF mints 100 "Condition A" tokens
    ↓
Splits into: 100 YES + 100 NO tokens
    ↓
Trades on market
    ↓
Event resolves → burns losers, redeems winners
```

### Revenue Model

**CTF Licensing:** Free and open-source[^143]

Gnosis does not earn direct revenue from CTF usage[^144]. Value accrues to:
- **GNO Token:** Governance and ecosystem growth[^145]
- **Omen Platform:** Trading fees (minimal volume vs Polymarket)[^146]
- **Gnosis Chain:** Validator fees[^147]

## Augur: The Pioneer

### Overview

**Augur** was the first decentralized prediction market protocol (launched 2018)[^163]. The DAO dissolved in 2023; the protocol remains operational but sees minimal activity[^164].

- **DAO:** Dissolved (2023)[^168]
- **Volume:** <$50M/month (minimal activity)[^169]
- **REP Market Cap:** 🔷 $7.9M (down from $500M peak)[^170]

### Why Augur Failed

1. **High Ethereum L1 Gas Fees:** $50-100 per trade during congestion[^171]
2. **Slow Resolution:** 7-day minimum dispute periods[^172]
3. **Complexity:** High barrier to entry for average users[^173]
4. **Liquidity Problems:** AMM model led to high slippage[^174]
5. **Controversial Markets:** Permissionless creation allowed assassination markets[^175]

**Lessons:** L1 Ethereum proved too expensive for prediction markets; L2s (Polygon) enabled viability[^176].
