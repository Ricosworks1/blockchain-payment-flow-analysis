# Sky (MakerDAO) Protocol Case Study: Dollar Distribution Analysis

## Overview

This case study analyzes how every dollar generated by Sky protocol[^1] (formerly MakerDAO)[^2] is distributed across core recipients based on official documentation[^1][^3] and on-chain data[^4][^5].

**Key Metrics (January 1, 2026):** 🔷 HARD DATA (retrieved via DefiLlama[^4] and CoinGecko[^5][^6][^7] APIs)
- **TVL:** ~$6.2B total[^4] (Ethereum $5.81B[^4], SKY Staking $414M[^4])
- **SKY Token:** $0.0602 | Market Cap: $1.38B[^5]
- **MKR Token:** $1,408.55 (deprecated, migrating to SKY)[^6]
- **DAI Supply:** $4.21B[^7]
- **USDS Supply:** Migrating from DAI[^1]
- **30d Fees:** $35M | Annual Run Rate: ~$420M[^8]
- **All-Time Revenue:** $972.6M[^8]
- **Architecture:** CDP (Collateralized Debt Positions)[^3]

## Revenue Structure

### Stability Fees[^9][^10]
- **Recipient**: Sky protocol surplus buffer[^11]
- **Mechanism**: 5-15% APR interest on DAI/USDS vault debt[^9]
- **Distribution**: Protocol accumulation then DSR/SSR payments[^12]

### DAI/USDS Savings Rate (DSR/SSR)[^12][^13]
- **Recipient**: DAI/USDS depositors[^12]
- **Mechanism**: 5-8% APY yield from stability fees[^13]
- **Distribution**: Automatic accrual to savers[^12]

### Liquidation Penalties[^14][^15]
- **Recipient**: Protocol surplus and keepers[^14]
- **Mechanism**: 13% penalty on liquidated vaults[^15]
- **Distribution**: ~85% to protocol[^11], ~15% to keepers[^14]

### Real World Asset (RWA) Yields[^16][^17]
- **Recipient**: Protocol surplus buffer[^11]
- **Mechanism**: T-bill yields (~4-5% APR)[^17]
- **Distribution**: Direct revenue to protocol[^16]

## Core Recipients Analysis

### 1. DAI/USDS Savings Rate Holders[^12][^13]
- **Revenue Sources**:
  - DSR/SSR yield (5-8% APY currently)[^13]
  - Funded by stability fees[^9]
  - RWA yield pass-through[^16]
- **Distribution Mechanism**:
  - Deposit DAI/USDS into savings contract[^12]
  - Automatic compound interest[^12]
  - Withdraw anytime[^13]
- **Current Allocation**: ~60-80% of protocol revenue[^18]

### 2. MKR/SKY Token Holders[^5][^6]
- **Revenue Sources**:
  - Surplus buffer overflow[^11]
  - Historical MKR burn (~$300M+ burned)[^19]
  - SKY farming rewards (6% rate)[^20]
- **Distribution Mechanism**:
  - MKR → SKY migration (1:24,000 ratio)[^21]
  - Token appreciation from burns[^19]
  - Governance voting rights[^22]
- **Market Cap**: $1.38B (SKY)[^5]

### 3. Vault Users (Borrowers)[^9][^23]
- **Revenue Sources**: None (they pay fees)
- **Costs**: Stability fees 5-15% APR[^9]
- **Benefits**: Capital-efficient borrowing[^23]
- **Collateral Types**: 30+ supported[^24]
- **Distribution**: Interest payments to protocol[^9]

### 4. SubDAO Allocators[^25][^26]
- **Revenue Sources**:
  - Allocated SKY rewards[^20]
  - Operating budgets[^27]
  - Performance incentives[^26]
- **Primary SubDAO**: Spark Protocol ($7.3B TVL)[^28]
- **Distribution**: Governance allocation via Stars[^25]

### 5. Core Units/Contributors[^27][^29]
- **Revenue Sources**:
  - Operating budgets (~$50-100M/year)[^27]
  - Development grants[^29]
  - Service agreements[^27]
- **Distribution Mechanism**:
  - Monthly budget disbursements[^27]
  - Governance-approved spending[^22]

### 6. Liquidation Keepers[^14][^30]
- **Revenue Sources**:
  - Liquidation incentives (~15% of penalty)[^14]
  - MEV opportunities[^30]
  - Dutch auction profits[^31]
- **Distribution Mechanism**:
  - Competitive auctions (Dog/Clipper)[^31]

### 7. RWA Partners[^16][^32]
- **Revenue Sources**:
  - Origination fees[^32]
  - Management fees (~50-100 bps)[^16]
  - Service agreements[^32]
- **Partners**: BlockTower Credit[^33], Huntingdon Valley Bank[^34], Monetalis[^35]
- **Distribution**: Legal structures and off-chain agreements[^32]

## Economic Flow Breakdown

### Revenue Generation Lifecycle[^3][^9][^16]
1. Users deposit collateral, open vaults[^23]
2. Mint DAI/USDS against collateral[^3]
3. Stability fees accrue on outstanding debt[^9]
4. RWA vaults generate T-bill yields[^17]
5. Fees flow to surplus buffer[^11]
6. DSR/SSR paid to savers from surplus[^12]
7. Excess surplus to MKR/SKY holders[^19][^20]

### Fee Distribution Percentages[^18]
- **DSR/SSR Holders**: ~60-80% of revenues[^12]
- **Protocol Surplus (Retained)**: ~20-40%[^11]
- **Core Units/Operations**: ~$50-100M annually[^27]
- **SubDAO Funding**: Variable via governance[^25]
- **MKR/SKY Holders**: Surplus overflow[^19]
