# Spark Protocol Case Study: Dollar Distribution Analysis

## Overview

This case study analyzes how every dollar generated by Spark Protocol[^1] (onchain capital allocator connected to Sky)[^21] is distributed across core recipients based on official documentation[^1] and on-chain data[^2][^5][^6].

**Key Metrics (December 31, 2025):** 🔷 HARD DATA (retrieved via DefiLlama[^2] and CoinGecko[^4] APIs)
- **TVL:** $7.3B total[^2] (Ethereum $6.66B[^2], Base $196M, Arbitrum $99M, Avalanche $200M)
- **SPK Token:** $0.021 | Market Cap: $46.4M | FDV: $210.5M[^4]
- **Circulating Supply:** 2.2B SPK (22%)[^4]
- **24h Volume:** $18.8M[^4]
- **Total Borrowed:** $1.39B[^2]
- **Chains:** Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Avalanche, Gnosis, Unichain[^2]

## Revenue Structure

### Interest Rate Spread[^24]
- **Recipient**: SparkDAO treasury (SubDAO of Sky)[^21][^42]
- **Mechanism**: Difference between borrow and supply rates[^24]
- **Distribution**: Reserve factor accumulation[^23]

### USDS Savings Rate Integration[^13]
- **Recipient**: USDS depositors[^13][^14]
- **Mechanism**: Direct savings rate pass-through from Sky[^13][^21]
- **Distribution**: Automatic yield to USDS suppliers[^14]

### Liquidation Penalties[^38]
- **Recipient**: Liquidators and protocol[^38]
- **Mechanism**: 5-10% penalty on liquidations[^38][^40]
- **Distribution**: Majority to liquidators, portion to treasury[^38][^42]

### Flash Loan Fees[^37]
- **Recipient**: Protocol treasury[^42]
- **Mechanism**: 0.09% fee on flash loans[^37]
- **Distribution**: 100% to protocol[^37]

## Economic Flow Breakdown

### Revenue Generation Lifecycle[^1][^24]
1. Users supply assets (especially USDS)[^44][^45]
2. USDS earns savings rate automatically[^13]
3. Borrowers pay interest rates[^43][^24]
4. Spread captured by protocol[^23]
5. Reserve factor to treasury[^42]
6. Liquidations generate penalties[^38]

### Fee Distribution Percentages[^23][^24]
- **Suppliers**: ~80-90% of interest revenue[^44]
- **SparkDAO Treasury**: ~10-20% reserve factor[^23]
- **Liquidators**: 100% of liquidation bonuses[^38]
- **Sky**: Governance control, indirect value[^21][^25]
