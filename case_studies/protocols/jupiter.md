# Jupiter Protocol Case Study: Dollar Distribution Analysis

## Executive Summary

Jupiter has established itself as Solana's dominant DEX aggregator and trading infrastructure, with $2.5 billion in TVL and over $1.47 billion in cumulative protocol fees since launch.[^1] The protocol's comprehensive product suite spans swap aggregation, perpetual futures, DCA automation, and limit orders, making it the primary trading interface for Solana users.[^2] This case study analyzes how every dollar generated by Jupiter is distributed across core recipients based on official documentation and on-chain data.

## Protocol Overview

Jupiter operates as a multi-product trading platform, routing swaps across all major Solana DEXs to achieve optimal execution for users.[^3] The protocol has processed over $100 billion in annual trading volume, capturing approximately 80%+ of Solana's aggregated swap market.[^4] Beyond aggregation, Jupiter's perpetual futures platform has emerged as a significant revenue driver, with JLP liquidity providers earning substantial yields from trader activity.[^5]

## Revenue Structure

### Swap Aggregation Fees

Jupiter charges minimal platform fees on aggregated swaps while providing users with best-execution routing across all Solana DEXs.[^9]

**Distribution:**
- **Recipient**: Jupiter protocol and referral partners
- **Mechanism**: Configurable fee on swap volume
- **Revenue Share**: Variable split with integrators (up to 50%)

### Limit Order Fees

The limit order product charges execution fees when orders are filled at specified prices.[^10]

**Distribution:**
- **Recipient**: Jupiter protocol treasury
- **Mechanism**: Fee on successfully executed orders
- **Revenue Share**: 100% to protocol

### DCA (Dollar Cost Averaging) Fees

Automated DCA execution generates recurring fee revenue from scheduled purchases.[^11]

**Distribution:**
- **Recipient**: Jupiter protocol and keepers
- **Mechanism**: Automation service fees per execution
- **Revenue Share**: Split between protocol and keepers

### Perpetuals Trading Fees

The Jupiter Perpetuals platform has emerged as a major revenue driver, with fees split between JLP providers and the protocol.[^12]

**Distribution:**
- **Recipient**: JLP pool (70%) and protocol (30%)
- **Mechanism**: Trading fees on leveraged positions
- **Revenue Share**: 70/30 split favoring LPs

### Bridge Aggregation Fees

Cross-chain bridge aggregation connects Solana to other ecosystems.[^13]

**Distribution:**
- **Recipient**: Jupiter and bridge partners
- **Mechanism**: Revenue sharing on bridge volume
- **Revenue Share**: Variable per bridge partner

## Economic Flow Breakdown

### Aggregation Revenue Lifecycle

**Stage 1: User Initiates Swap**
- User specifies trade parameters
- Jupiter calculates optimal routes[^31]

**Stage 2: Route Optimization**
- Multi-DEX pathfinding
- Split trade calculation
- Price impact minimization

**Stage 3: Execution**
- Atomic transaction across DEXs
- MEV protection applied[^29]
- Best execution achieved

**Stage 4: Fee Distribution**
- Platform fee collected
- Referrer share distributed
- Treasury accumulation

### Perpetuals Revenue Flow

**Stage 1: Position Opening**
- Trader deposits collateral
- Leverage selected (up to 100x)[^32]
- Position established

**Stage 2: Fee Collection**
- Opening fee charged
- Borrowing fees accrue
- Funding rates collected/paid

**Stage 3: Revenue Distribution**
- 70% to JLP pool[^12]
- 30% to protocol treasury
- JLP token appreciates

**Stage 4: Position Resolution**
- Close fees charged
- Liquidation profits to JLP if applicable
- Final PnL settled
