# Meteora Protocol Case Study: Dollar Distribution Analysis

## Overview

This case study analyzes how every dollar generated by Meteora protocol[^1] (Solana DEX and liquidity platform)[^25] is distributed across core recipients based on official documentation[^1] and on-chain data[^2][^5][^6].

## Revenue Structure

### Dynamic Liquidity Market Maker (DLMM) Fees[^7]
- **Recipient**: Liquidity providers[^7]
- **Mechanism**: Variable fees based on volatility (0.01-2%)[^21][^36]
- **Distribution**: 100% to LPs in active bins[^7][^20]

### Dynamic Vaults Fees[^13]
- **Recipient**: Vault depositors and protocol[^13]
- **Mechanism**: Performance fees on yield strategies[^13][^50]
- **Distribution**: Majority to depositors, performance fee to protocol[^13]

### Dynamic AMM Pools Fees[^14]
- **Recipient**: Liquidity providers[^14]
- **Mechanism**: Standard swap fees (0.25-1%)[^14][^36]
- **Distribution**: Pro-rata to LPs[^14]

### Memecoin Launchpad Fees[^22]
- **Recipient**: Meteora protocol[^22]
- **Mechanism**: Launch fees and trading commissions[^22][^46]
- **Distribution**: Platform revenue[^22]

## Economic Flow Breakdown

### DLMM Fee Lifecycle[^7][^20]
1. Trader executes swap[^3][^19]
2. Dynamic fee calculated based on volatility[^21][^36]
3. Fee distributed to active bins only[^20][^42]
4. LPs earn concentrated rewards[^7][^41]
5. No protocol fee extraction[^7]
6. MET incentives on top[^47][^48]

### Fee Distribution Percentages[^1][^7]
- **DLMM LPs**: 100% of swap fees[^7][^20]
- **Vault Depositors**: ~90-95% of strategy yields[^13][^50]
- **Protocol Treasury**: ~5-10% performance fees[^13][^54]
- **MET Stakers**: Future revenue share[^23][^48]
