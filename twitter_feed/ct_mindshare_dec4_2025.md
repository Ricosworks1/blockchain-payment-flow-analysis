## Methodology & Limitations

This analysis examines Crypto Twitter (CT) discourse on December 4, 2025, distinguishing between **verified facts**, **unverified claims**, and **potential manipulation signals**.

### Critical Framework

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| ✅ VERIFIED | Confirmed via official sources, on-chain data, or reputable news |
| ⚠️ UNVERIFIED | Claims circulating on CT without independent confirmation |
| 🚩 MANIPULATION RISK | Potential bot activity, coordinated shilling, or misleading narratives |

### Bot Activity Warning

Research indicates **9-15% of active Twitter accounts are bots**[^1], with crypto-specific manipulation showing **20-50% "inauthentic chatter"** for promoted tokens[^2]. Higher bot probability correlates with **lower long-term returns**—one study found a token with the highest bot activity saw a **95% price decrease within three months**[^3].

## Manipulation Indicators

### 🚩 Red Flags Identified

1. **Airdrop spam**: Repetitive posts with identical language (bot pattern)
2. **Inflated engagement claims**: "200+ likes" without verification
3. **Fabricated data**: Treasury buyback figure ($12.5B) not matching official sources
4. **Overstated price movements**: ETH "+6.8%" vs. verified +4%
5. **Project shilling**: Specific tokens mentioned without verifiable fundamentals

### Bot Activity Correlation

| Pattern | Historical Outcome |
|---------|-------------------|
| High bot probability tokens | 95% price decrease within 3 months[^3] |
| Bot spikes preceding price moves | 11-30% single-day pumps followed by dumps[^2] |
| "Inauthentic chatter" growth | 20% → 50% for manipulated tokens[^2] |
