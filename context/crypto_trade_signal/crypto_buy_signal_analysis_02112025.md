## 1. MVRV Z-Score: Market Value to Realized Value

### Methodology

The MVRV Z-Score is a statistically enhanced on-chain metric that compares Bitcoin's market value to its realized value, adjusted for historical volatility. It identifies periods of extreme overvaluation (potential tops) and undervaluation (potential bottoms).

**Formula:**
```
MVRV Z-Score = (Market Cap - Realized Cap) / Standard Deviation of Market Cap
```

### Signal Thresholds

**Overvaluation Signals (Sell Zone):**
- **Z-Score > 7:** Extreme overvaluation driven by speculative euphoria
- Historical accuracy: Market tops identified within 2 weeks across multiple cycles
- Past cycle peaks: Reached 10 (twice), 8 (twice), and 7 (once)

**Undervaluation Signals (Buy Zone):**
- **Z-Score < 0:** Market value far below realized value
- Historical buy opportunities: Late 2018 and 2022
- Green zone entries have produced outsized returns

- **Interpretation:** Mid-cycle positioning with recent consolidation after October ATH

- **Key Level:** Breaking above 3 with established support could signal next major price discovery phase

### 2025 Projections [UPDATED]

## 2. Pi Cycle Top Indicator: Moving Average Crossover

### Methodology

The Pi Cycle Top Indicator predicts Bitcoin market cycle highs by identifying crossovers between two moving averages:
- **Fast MA:** 111-day moving average
- **Slow MA:** 2 × 350-day moving average

**Signal Generation:** When the 111-day MA crosses above the 2 × 350-day MA, it generates a potential market top signal.

### Historical Performance

**Successful Signals:**
- **2013 Bull Market Top:** Predicted within 3 days
- **2017 Bull Market Top:** Predicted within 3 days
- **2021 Bull Market Top:** Predicted within 3 days (first peak)

## 3. NVT Ratio: Network Value to Transactions

### Methodology

The NVT Ratio is the cryptocurrency equivalent of a Price-to-Earnings (P/E) ratio, substituting transaction volume as the key metric since Bitcoin doesn't generate earnings.

**Formula:**
```
NVT Ratio = Market Cap (USD) / Daily Transaction Volume (USD)
```

### Signal Interpretation

**High NVT Ratio:**
- Overvaluation indicator
- Historically coincides with market tops
- Network value growing faster than utility

**Low NVT Ratio:**
- Undervaluation indicator
- Historically marks accumulation periods
- Often coincides with market bottoms and trending bull markets

## 4. Puell Multiple: Miner Revenue Analysis

### Methodology

The Puell Multiple examines Bitcoin miner revenues (in USD) relative to their 365-day average, signaling when price may be overextended or undervalued relative to mining fundamentals.

**Formula:**
```
Puell Multiple = Daily Coin Issuance (USD) / 365-Day MA of Daily Coin Issuance
```

### Signal Thresholds

**Overvaluation Zone:**
- **Puell Multiple > 4:** Historically corresponds to market peaks
- Miners earning significantly above average
- Often signals unsustainable price levels

**Undervaluation Zone:**
- **Puell Multiple < 0.5:** Marks miner stress and market bottoms
- Miners under extreme revenue pressure
- Often presents accumulation opportunities

- Miner revenues in line with past year's average, supporting price sustainability
