## 5. Risk Management Framework

### **Subsidy Runway Analysis**

For any subsidized protocol, calculate:

**Formula:** `Runway (years) = Foundation Treasury ÷ Annual Burn Rate`

**Example:**
- Solana Foundation: $1B treasury, $200M/year burn = **5-year runway** ✅
- Random L2: $50M treasury, $100M/year burn = **0.5-year runway** ❌

**Decision Rule:**
- Runway >3 years: Safe to hold through bear market
- Runway 1-3 years: Momentum play only, exit before cycle top
- Runway <1 year: Avoid or short

### **Token Unlock Risk**

**From Token Unlock Research:**
- $20-28B/year in scheduled unlocks = massive sell pressure
- Many protocols have 50-80% of supply still locked

**Decision Rule:**
- Check unlock schedule before entry (use Token Unlocks case studies)
- Avoid positions 3-6 months before major unlock events
- Exit or hedge during large unlock periods

### **Foundation Transparency Score**

**Green Flags:**
- Quarterly treasury reports (e.g., Ethereum Foundation)
- On-chain governance with public votes
- Clear grant allocation and ROI metrics

**Red Flags:**
- Opaque foundation spending
- No public treasury audits
- "Trust us" governance model
